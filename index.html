  • Commvault expanded from file-share-only Threat Scan (2023E) to hash and YARA hunting, Deep Inspection and Synthetic Recovery, all generally available by the first half of 2026.
  • Cohesity announced Google Threat Intelligence for threat scanning at no extra cost in its Enterprise edition (September 2025), and added Sophos antivirus scanning in its Enterprise edition (March 2026).

Customer Problem / Challenges

Customer Problems and Challenges

Missed detection

Users are often surprised that Threat Monitoring covers only about 30 file extensions and a maximum file size of 15MB. When instructed to use Threat Hunting, the setup is much more complicated, which leads to human error.

Seen in the field

Keven Miller ran a lab Threat Hunt for the MD5 of a file already flagged by Threat Monitoring returned no matches, because the file's .zip extension was missing from the hunt's default inclusion list. Using * avoids the miss, though a hunt can then take hours per snapshot.

Resource usage and scan control

CDM customers occasionally identify Threat Monitoring YARA full scans as large resource consumers, generally when dense clusters are nearing capacity. Customers cannot control how intensively objects are scanned, and the lack of CDM sizing adds to the exposure.

Seen in the field

Customers ask about CPU and storage impact before enabling Threat Monitoring. One cluster with under 10% free disk paused onboarding until the storage overhead was explained.

False positive verification

False positive storms continue to be handled by product management and engineering through ad-hoc removal of rules. Delayed Detection and Detection Management help, and customers still look to Rubrik for a final verdict. Today there is no offering for it.

Seen in the field

Support cases ask to suppress recurring alerts without disabling an indicator globally, for example about 42 flagged objects awaiting validation on one account.

RRT cases and EE trials require experts

RRT cases commonly show users waiting on IR experts to provide IOCs. EE trials are unlikely to find value unless a leading IR team is involved.

Seen in the field

RRT guidance makes IOC availability the condition for enabling an EE trial. One customer held off on Threat Hunting because no external security vendor was available to supply IOCs.

Next steps

Next Steps and Product Management Needs

Product Management plan

  • Prioritize AV-like enhancements that affect POC outcomes: archive expansion, file size and type scope, fuzzy hash for variant detection, YARA coverage across all clouds, and Detection Management Phase 2.
  • Identify detection partners to close gaps in heuristic, machine-learning and sandbox detection. Options: Google Threat Intelligence private scanning and sandbox, in-house machine-learning detection, and a licensed antivirus engine.
  • Equip the field with updated POC test plans for added strengths. Provide sizing / speed metrics to hold value / ensure customer satisfaction post-sale.
  • Identify "Rubrik Heuristic" opportunities to corroborate filesystem threats based on data and identity (i.e. data ownership).

Product Management Needs

1. Reduce delivery friction for RSC-G and RSC-P

Core capabilities have taken up to three years to reach RSC-P. RSC-G delays have improved to about six months. Support a delivery model that ships RSC-G and RSC-P parity with the commercial release.

2. Platform adoption of Orion capabilities

Workload and deployment coverage accounts for 21 of 60 Orion releases since FY24Q3 (35%). Orion currently funds each workload integration. Continue promoting a model in which platform and workload teams adopt Orion capabilities through the shared framework as part of their own delivery.

Opportunities · Partnerships

3rd Party Integration Overview

Three ways to use partner technology, with what each gives and what each costs.

Enrichment

Show partner alerts on each protected object to help pick a clean recovery point.

Pros

  • Fastest to build: the identity alert pipeline and a Defender connector already exist.
  • Customers keep the security tools they already own.
  • Closes the gap Commvault opened with CrowdStrike (January 2025).

Cons

  • Shows partner verdicts only, with no new detection by Rubrik.
  • Each platform needs its own object matching.
  • Needs the customer to license the partner product.

Detection Automation

A partner alert triggers a Rubrik hunt, then the threat is excluded on recovery.

Pros

  • Turns a partner alert into a recovery outcome with no manual hunting.
  • Matches Commvault's Falcon-triggered scans and Charlotte SOAR actions.
  • Partly built: the Cortex pack already returns the last recoverable snapshot.

Cons

  • Most likely outcome is proving a negative: no malware found due to deletion from source.

Enhanced Detection

License technology that improves Rubrik's own detection: scan engines, file analysis, rules.

Pros

  • Closes the 0-day gap that signatures cannot.
  • Works without the customer owning a third-party tool.
  • Offline options for RSC-P.

Cons

  • Highest effort: embedding, licensing cost at scale.
Sources: partner research (enrichment, detection automation, AV engine OEM); Orion roadmap12 / 15
